# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes agricultural performance across different **seasons** (Kharif & Rabi) in India, investigating how environmental conditions, farming practices, and resource management impact crop yield, productivity, and profitability. The analysis identifies seasonal patterns, trends, and actionable insights for farmers and stakeholders.

## 📊 Dataset
- **Source**: Seasonal agriculture dataset covering farms across multiple Indian states
- **Records**: Comprehensive farm-level data including Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, Telangana, and others
- **Seasons Covered**: Kharif (monsoon) & Rabi (winter)
- **Key Variables** (27 attributes):
  - **Environmental**: Rainfall, Temperature, Humidity, Sunlight hours, Soil pH, Soil Moisture
  - **Resources**: Irrigation method, Fertilizer, Pesticide, Seed quality
  - **Nutrients**: Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium application rates
  - **Performance**: Yield, Production, Market price, Profitability, Water efficiency

## 🔍 Key Analysis Performed
**Seasonal Performance Comparison**: Yield & profitability by season (Kharif vs Rabi)  
**Crop-wise Analysis**: Performance variation across crops (Wheat, Maize, Pulses, Rice, etc.)  
**State-wise Trends**: Regional differences in agricultural output  
**Environmental Impact**: How rainfall, temperature, and soil conditions affect yield  
**Resource Optimization**: Efficiency of irrigation methods & fertilizer usage  
**Profitability Analysis**: Cost vs Revenue patterns across seasons  
**Risk Assessment**: Disease/pest risk patterns by season

## 💡 Key Findings (Summary)
- Seasonal variations significantly impact crop yield and farmer profitability
- Environmental factors (rainfall, temperature) play crucial role in performance
- Irrigation efficiency and nutrient management vary by season
- Risk of pest/disease shows seasonal patterns requiring preventive strategies
